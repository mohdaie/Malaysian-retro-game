How to play this practice round

Choose one of your seven houses on the bottom row. Sow shells counterclockwise, one per house, including your own store and skipping Pak Mat's store. If the last shell lands in a house with shells, pick them all up and keep sowing. Finish in your store for another turn. Finish in an empty house on your side to capture the opposite shells, if any. When one row is empty, the remaining shells go to their owner's store.

This is a turn-based introductory variant with seven shells per house. Regional and simultaneous-start rules vary.
